Offense types are assigned by keyword from the official offense description, using each grant's most serious count, and have not been reviewed record by record. A drug or violent offense counts as white collar only when a financial-deception count, such as money laundering, is also present.

White-collar status comes from the full rubric. Offense types are a rougher layer: assigned by keyword from the official offense description (most serious count) and not reviewed record by record, so treat them as approximate.
